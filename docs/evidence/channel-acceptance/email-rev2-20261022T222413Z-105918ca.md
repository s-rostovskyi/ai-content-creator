Subject: Packable warmth for everyday plans

Preheader: Lightweight, water-resistant, and ready to stash when the day changes.

Body:  
Meet the Men’s Nano Puff Jacket, style 84212—a regular-fit layer built for days that move between town and trail. It’s lightweight, highly compressible, and designed to pack down when you don’t need it, then pull back out when you do.

The shell is 100% recycled polyester ripstop with a DWR finish made without intentionally added PFAS; Patagonia says that finish is meant to shed light moisture. The insulation is 60-g PrimaLoft® Gold Insulation Eco made from 100% postconsumer recycled polyester, and the jacket includes brick quilting to help keep the insulation in place. Practical details include a center-front zipper with an interior storm flap and chin zipper garage, two zippered handwarmer pockets, and an internal zippered chest pocket that doubles as a stuffsack.

You’ll also find a drawcord-adjustable drop-tail hem and a regular fit for easy layering. For care, Patagonia lists machine wash warm, do not bleach, tumble dry low, and do not iron.
