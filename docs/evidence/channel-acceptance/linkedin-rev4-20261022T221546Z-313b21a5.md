The Men’s Nano Puff® Jacket, style 84212, is built around a practical idea: keep the profile low and the function high.

Patagonia describes it as warm, windproof, water-resistant, lightweight, and highly compressible. That last point matters in the field and on the move: the internal zippered chest pocket doubles as a stuffsack, so the jacket can stow away when the weather shifts. The rest of the design stays equally focused—brick quilting, a center-front zipper with an interior storm flap and chin zipper garage, two zippered handwarmer pockets, and a drawcord-adjustable drop-tail hem.

Material details are specific, too: a 100% recycled polyester ripstop shell with a DWR finish made without intentionally added PFAS, plus 60-g PrimaLoft® Gold Insulation Eco made from 100% postconsumer recycled polyester.
