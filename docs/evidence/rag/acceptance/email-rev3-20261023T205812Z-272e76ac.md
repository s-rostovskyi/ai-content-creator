Subject: Men’s Nano Puff Jacket: packable warmth

Preheader: Lightweight, windproof, and easy to stash for everyday outings.

Body:
Meet the Men’s Nano Puff® Jacket, style 84212: a regular-fit layer built for cool days, windy walks, and travel-ready layering. It’s lightweight and highly compressible, so it packs down easily when you want a jacket that won’t take up much space.

The jacket is described as warm, windproof, and water-resistant. It uses a 100% recycled polyester ripstop shell with a DWR finish made without intentionally added PFAS, and PrimaLoft® Gold Insulation Eco for low-bulk insulation. Practical details include a center-front zipper with an interior storm flap and chin zipper garage, two zippered handwarmer pockets, an internal zippered chest pocket that doubles as a stuffsack, and a drawcord-adjustable drop-tail hem.

For everyday outings, that pocket-to-stuffsack detail offers a practical way to keep the jacket packed between uses.

For easy care, Patagonia lists machine wash warm, no bleach, tumble dry low, and no iron. Explore the product details to see whether it fits your everyday routine and outdoor plans.
