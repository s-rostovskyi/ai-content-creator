Patagonia’s Men’s Nano Puff® Jacket, style 84212, is built for days when you want warmth without a lot of bulk.

Its lightweight, highly compressible design makes it easy to bring along for everyday outings and outdoor trips. A key detail is the internal zippered chest pocket, which doubles as a stuffsack, so the jacket can pack down into itself when you want to save space. Other practical features include a center-front zipper with an interior storm flap and chin zipper garage, two zippered handwarmer pockets, and a drawcord-adjustable drop-tail hem.

The jacket is also listed as warm, windproof, and water-resistant. It uses a shell with a DWR finish made without intentionally added PFAS.
